# Closet model visuals

Created with the built-in image-generation tool using the user's uploaded Jean photo as the garment reference.

Output: `closet-model-looks.png`. The image is also embedded in `../index.html`, so the HTML can be opened without a separate asset folder.

The first two panels visualise the uploaded dark indigo wide-leg jeans. All complementary garments are suggestions, not owned items or verified retailer products. The third panel is entirely inspiration. The models are AI-generated, not photographs of people physically wearing the garment. Fabric and fit are approximate visualisations.

These are three prepared images for this reference garment, not an automatic image-generation backend. The app checks the item ID and photo fingerprint before using them. A different garment requires new imagery. No credentials or paid image API are embedded in the HTML.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one high quality fashion editorial triptych image for a closet app, exactly THREE equal-width vertical panels, side by side, no gaps or borders, overall landscape 1536x1024. Each panel contains ONE full-length adult male fashion model from head to entire shoes, centred with generous margins so no body parts are cut off. All three have warm pale stone studio backdrops and natural soft editorial lighting, realistic human faces, skin and fabric, premium menswear lookbook photography. LEFT PANEL: adult male model wearing the EXACT jeans from the reference photograph: deep dark indigo blue, subtle wash, very wide straight legs, high waist, five pocket construction, contrast tan seams, no rips. Preserve this garment faithfully including width and colour. Style with a white heavyweight crewneck tee tucked in, white minimal low-top sneakers, no jacket. CENTRE PANEL: a different adult male model wearing the SAME exact reference jeans, preserve wide straight legs dark indigo denim, seams and wash, styled with a fine black knit polo, open lightweight warm beige tailored blazer, dark brown leather loafers. RIGHT PANEL: third adult male model wearing a sage green relaxed long-sleeve camp collar shirt, stone tailored pleated trousers, brown suede loafers, no denim. This third outfit is entirely inspiration. Natural confident relaxed poses, hands not obscuring the jeans details. All models fully clothed. Absolutely no text, logos, typography, watermarks, illustrated clothing, flat lays, floating garments, or extra people. Exactly three equal vertical panels with full-length human models and feet visible.
