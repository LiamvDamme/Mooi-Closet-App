# MOOI v9 — free improvements and remaining connections

Implemented without adding a paid service:

- Free wardrobe mixes use only available owned items. Clothing type, colour, locked pieces and no-heels choices are enforced. Saved favourites, wear counts and negative feedback influence selection. With a small wardrobe, fewer than three distinct combinations may be possible; repeats are identified. This is a rules-based option, not AI.
- AI styling receives saved looks, recent wears, feedback and the new quick choices. Paid images are optional and off by default. Generation runs while the user browses; keep the tab open. Database locks are released during AI network calls, then results merge into the latest account state. Changed references invalidate old results. Closing the tab, signing out or a free-host restart can interrupt progress; retry from the saved outfit. This is not a durable worker queue.
- Plain-background cleaning runs in the browser with an original-photo restore button. It is suitable for evenly lit plain backgrounds, not general-purpose AI segmentation. Similar-photo warnings use local perceptual hashes on new uploads; exact duplicates are also checked. AI tagging shows low-confidence warnings and stops automatic retries after an unavailable-service error. Batch review remains one photo at a time.
- Real garment collages appear immediately. Missing garment photos use labelled tiles instead of pretending a model image exists. Shoe-only swaps are one click away in the outfit.
- The daily overview includes existing live weather, a planned or available saved look, an alternative and calendar event notes. Suggestions ask users to review the weather; they are not automatically weather-verified recommendations.
- Accepted friends can vote on optional outfit polls, save inspiration and create wardrobe versions. Matching rejects category-only matches. Blocking removes friendships and hides both users from each other's search/feed. Reports go to an owner-only review queue; the owner can review or remove the reported post.
- Product catalogue entries support stock status, relevant name ranking and stale-check warnings after seven days. Out-of-stock listings are excluded. Prices and sizes are never invented. No live retailer feed or partnership has been connected, so retailer searches remain labelled searches; import only product data you have checked and are permitted to use.
- Owners can export an AES-256-GCM encrypted database backup from Profile, using their account password and a separate 16+ character backup passphrase. Active sessions are excluded. Keep backups and passphrases separately; loss of the passphrase cannot be recovered. `backups.js` exports the authenticated decryptor for an administrator restoring to a prepared database. No restore was performed, and no account data was downloaded during development. Users retain individual wardrobe export/import and password recovery codes.

Not enabled: email verification and emailed password-reset links (no mail delivery service configured), automated off-site database backups, large-scale object storage, live retailer feeds, unrestricted AI background removal, or additional AI credits. No credit purchase or new paid plan was made.

Database: keep the existing free Render database per the owner's instruction. Its previously reported expiry is 23 October 2026. An encrypted backup does not extend its life. Arrange a free replacement and migration before expiry.

Validation: 23 automated checks, including account isolation, concurrent edits during image rendering, free generation constraints, encryption, polls, reporting, blocking, photos, calendar, catalogue, recovery and existing AI request behaviour. Browser checks cover the free generator and calendar events. No live paid AI call is required for these tests.
